About me

I am a QA Engineer, certified as a QA Analyst, specializing in manual and automated testing for web and mobile applications.

I have executed over 380 tests across manual, API, automation, and mobile environments, reporting more than 100 defects in Jira. My experience includes designing flowcharts, test cases, equivalence classes, and checklists, as well as conducting requirement analysis to ensure system functionality, data architecture consistency, and user value delivery. I apply tools such as Python, Selenium, Pytest and SQL to validate data and automate critical flows.

Beyond software testing, I have leveraged AI to generate AutoLISP routines in AutoCAD, optimizing and ensuring 100% accuracy in piping layouts and accessories. I also developed routines to automatically label each component and generate inventory files, reducing manual errors and improving traceability in industrial projects.

I bring an industrial engineering mindset into QA. My background in Lean methodology, process auditing, and project coordination enables me to ensure not only that software works, but that processes are efficient, scalable, and aligned with continuous improvement principles.

I focus on both testing software and improving workflows, which allows me to: - Identify inefficiencies and propose Lean-driven solutions. - Coordinate projects with technical and business stakeholders. - Audit processes to ensure compliance and continuous improvement.

I am passionate about learning and applying new technologies, exploring digital tools that enhance efficiency and innovation. My goal is to combine technical precision with analytical thinking to deliver reliable solutions and outstanding user experiences.
